# ADR-0001 — Platform Name

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-06-25

---

## Context

The project started as a backend for importing CueScore tournaments and calculating player ratings.

As the project evolved, its purpose expanded beyond ratings.

The Platform now aims to preserve, organize and present the complete competitive history of American Pool in Greece.

The project therefore required an official identity that reflects its long-term vision.

---

## Decision

The official name of the Platform is:

# Hellenic American Pool History

Official slogan:

> Preserving the History of Greek American Pool

---

## Rationale

The name was selected because it clearly communicates:

- Geographic scope (Hellenic)
- Sport (American Pool)
- Purpose (History)

The Platform is not limited to ratings.

Its primary mission is to become the historical archive of competitive American Pool in Greece.

---

## Consequences

Future development should always support this mission.

Every major feature should contribute to one or more of the following:

- Historical preservation
- Accurate player information
- Tournament history
- Reliable statistics
- Player ratings
- Community value

Features that do not contribute to the mission should be reconsidered or postponed.

---

## Principles

The Platform values:

- Accuracy over speed
- Simplicity over complexity
- Maintainability over shortcuts
- Long-term sustainability
- Community first

---

## Notes

This ADR establishes the identity of the Platform and serves as the foundation for future architectural decisions.
