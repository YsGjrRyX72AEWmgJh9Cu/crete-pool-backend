# Hellenic American Pool History

> **Preserving the History of Greek American Pool**

---

# Mission

The Hellenic American Pool History Platform exists to preserve, organize and present the complete competitive history of American Pool in Greece.

Its purpose is to create a reliable historical archive for players, clubs, tournament organizers and future generations.

---

# Vision

To become the reference historical archive and statistics platform for American Pool in Greece.

---

# Core Values

- Accuracy over speed
- Historical preservation
- Clean architecture
- Transparency
- Long-term sustainability
- Community first

---

# Scope

The first version of the Platform focuses exclusively on:

- American Pool 8-ball
- American Pool 9-ball
- American Pool 10-ball

Other cue sports may be supported in the future without changing the Platform architecture.

---

# Long-term Goals

- Preserve tournament history
- Preserve player history
- Reliable player ratings
- Historical statistics
- Tournament statistics
- Head-to-head analysis
- Performance analytics
- Public API
- Modern web platform

---

# Philosophy

Every Match Matters.

Every Player Has a Story.

Every Tournament Becomes History.
