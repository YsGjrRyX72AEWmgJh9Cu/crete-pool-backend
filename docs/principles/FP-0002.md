# Foundation Principle FP-0002

# Preserve History

## Principle

We preserve history because history teaches us.

History corrects our mistakes and moves us forward.

The Hellenic American Pool History Platform preserves not only the history of Greek American Pool, but also its own evolution.

Previous versions, architectural decisions, documentation, and lessons learned are valuable historical records.

Nothing is discarded simply because it is old.

History provides context.

Context produces understanding.

Understanding leads to better decisions.

---

## Why

The mission of the platform is historical preservation.

That mission also applies to the platform itself.

By preserving our own evolution, we make future development easier, safer, and more understandable.

Every previous implementation tells part of the story.

Every refactoring represents learning.

Every architectural decision explains why the platform became what it is.

---

## Practical Consequences

This principle encourages us to:

- Preserve historical documentation.
- Archive previous implementations instead of deleting them.
- Record architectural decisions.
- Document the reasons behind important changes.
- Learn from previous versions instead of hiding them.

---

## Final Statement

History is not a burden.

History is knowledge.

Knowledge moves the platform forward.

> "History corrects our mistakes and moves us forward."
>
> — Founding Reflection of the Hellenic American Pool History Platform
