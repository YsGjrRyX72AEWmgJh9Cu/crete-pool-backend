# Purpose

This document captures ideas, principles and future directions that emerge during the design of the Hellenic American Pool History Platform.

Not every good idea should be implemented immediately.

Some ideas need time to mature.

The Vision Backlog ensures that valuable ideas are never lost while allowing the platform to evolve deliberately and consistently.
