# Review Checklist

Before approving a specification, verify that:

- The entity supports the platform's mission.
- The specification is independent of implementation details.
- Historical principles are clearly defined.
- Design principles are documented.
- Components are identified before fields.
- Every field has a historical purpose.
- Every field has a business purpose.
- The specification is consistent with the Domain Model.
- The specification is consistent with the Project Constitution.
