# Vision

# Why this project exists

The Hellenic American Pool History project exists to preserve the history of Hellenic American pocket billiards and to ensure that this history remains accessible, understandable and expandable for future generations.

The project is more than a software application.

It is a long-term digital heritage platform dedicated to documenting players, tournaments, venues, organizations, matches and historical achievements with accuracy, integrity and respect for the history of the sport.

---

# Our Mission

Our mission is to build a reliable and maintainable platform that preserves the historical record of Hellenic American pocket billiards while providing future generations with the knowledge required to understand, maintain and continue its evolution.

The project aims to serve players, organizers, researchers, historians and future software developers alike.

---

# Our Philosophy

The project is guided by two equally important responsibilities.

## Preserve History

Every tournament tells a story.

Every match preserves a memory.

Every player becomes part of history.

Historical information should never be treated as temporary data.

It represents the legacy of a sporting community and deserves to be preserved with care, accuracy and integrity.

---

## Preserve Knowledge

Software documentation is as valuable as software itself.

Future developers should be able to understand not only how the software works, but also why it was designed this way.

Architecture, business rules and technical decisions should always be documented.

Knowledge preservation enables long-term sustainability.

---

# Long-Term Vision

This project is designed to outlive its original authors.

Every architectural decision should make the platform easier to understand, maintain and evolve many years from now.

Success is measured not only by the software we build today, but by the ability of future generations to confidently continue building upon it.
