# The Constitution of the Platform

# Hellenic American Pool History

> **Preserving the History of Greek American Pool**

---

## Why does this Constitution exist?

The Platform is expected to evolve for many years.

Technology will change.

Programming languages will change.

Frameworks will change.

The codebase will grow.

The Constitution exists to preserve the identity of the Platform regardless of how the technology evolves.

---

## What does the Constitution define?

The Constitution defines:

* the mission of the Platform
* its values
* its architectural principles
* the way decisions are documented
* the responsibilities of future contributors

---

## Our Promise

The Platform exists to serve the American Pool community.

Every decision must support the mission.

No feature is more important than the integrity of the historical archive.

---

## Guiding Principle

> We do not write code to complete tasks.

> We write code to serve the mission of the Hellenic American Pool History Platform.

---

## Long-term Vision

To become the trusted historical archive of competitive American Pool in Greece.

The Constitution should remain valid even if every line of software is rewritten.
