# Constitutional Principle CP-0001

# Documentation is Part of the Platform

**Status:** Permanent

**Established:** 2026-06-25

---

## Principle

Documentation is not written after development.

Documentation is development.

Every important decision becomes part of the Platform documentation.

Nothing important lives only inside conversations.

---

## Why

Knowledge must belong to the Platform, not to its creators.

The Platform should remain understandable even if its original creators are no longer involved.

Documentation preserves the identity, purpose and evolution of the Platform.

---

## Commitment

Every important:

- architectural decision
- foundation principle
- constitutional principle
- milestone
- roadmap update
- strategic idea

must be documented.

If it is important, it becomes part of the Platform.

---

## Our Responsibility

Future contributors should be able to understand:

- why a decision was made
- how the Platform evolved
- what principles guided its development

without needing to ask the original creators.

---

## Motto

> Documentation is part of the product.

> Nothing important lives only inside conversations.

---

## This Principle Exists Because

The Hellenic American Pool History Platform is intended to serve the American Pool community for many years.

Documentation ensures that the Platform can continue to evolve, regardless of who develops it in the future.
